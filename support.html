Lumvia — Support

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Not pastoral, medical or mental-health care. Lumvia offers Bible verses and an AI-written reflection and prayer. It is not a substitute for a pastor, doctor or therapist. In a crisis, call your local emergency services or a helpline (US: 988).

Questions, a billing issue, or feedback? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we answer within two business days. Please include your iOS version, the app version, and what happened (do not send private prayers).

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lumvia → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Restore purchases

Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the paywall.

Crisis

US: call or text 988. Elsewhere, contact local emergency services or a local helpline.
