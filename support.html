Lumvia help

Answers to the things people ask us

Q.An answer will not load

It needs an internet connection, because the question goes to our server. The daily verse and your saved answers work offline. Try again in a minute, or check that the consent screen was accepted.

Q.Restoring Pro

Open Settings inside Lumvia and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Q.Cancelling

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lumvia. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the paid period.

Q.Anything else

Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.
