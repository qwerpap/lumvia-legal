Lumvia privacy

What happens to the things you write and read here

i.Before anything else

Lumvia gives you Bible verses and an AI-written reflection and prayer. It is not a pastor, doctor or therapist. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a helpline (in the US, call or text 988).

ii.Your questions leave the phone

When you ask something, the app first shows a consent screen. After you agree, it sends the text you typed, your language, your chosen prayer tradition and an optional topic to a server that the developer runs on Google Cloud Functions. That server forwards it to an AI model provider (Google Gemini through Replicate, with OpenAI as a fallback) so the model can pick Bible references and write a short reflection and prayer.

The model only chooses references. The verse wording shown to you is read from the Bible stored in the app. We do not use your questions for advertising and do not sell them. Please leave out names and details you would not want processed by an AI service.

iii.What never leaves the phone

  • Your saved verses, past answers and streak, kept in a local database.
  • The Bible text itself (World English Bible, King James Version, Reina-Valera 1909, Bíblia Portuguesa Mundial — all public domain).
  • Prayers read aloud: speech is generated by the voice built into iOS, on the device.

iv.Reminders and pictures

Daily-verse reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; there is no push server. For verse cards, Lumvia asks for add-only access to Photos and uses it only when you tap Save. It cannot read your library.

v.Purchases

Apple processes payments. Adapty (adapty.io) receives your purchase and subscription status and anonymous usage events such as onboarding steps and paywall views, linked to a random profile ID. We do not collect your name or email, and there are no accounts.

vi.Children, deletion, contact

Lumvia is not aimed at children under 13. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone; photos you saved stay in Photos. To ask about data held for your anonymous Adapty profile, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com.
