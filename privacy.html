Lumvia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Not pastoral, medical or mental-health care. Lumvia offers Bible verses and an AI-written reflection and prayer. It is not a substitute for a pastor, doctor or therapist. In a crisis, call your local emergency services or a helpline (US: 988).

Lumvia (bundle com.lf.lumvia, developer Danil) is built to keep your data on your device. There is no account and no login.

What stays on your device

Your saved items and previous answers are stored only on your device in a local database.

Questions you ask

Before your first question the app shows a consent screen. When you ask a question, the text you typed, plus your chosen language, prayer tradition and topic, is sent to our server (Google Cloud Functions, in a project hosted by the developer) and from there to an AI model provider (Google Gemini via Replicate; OpenAI as a fallback) to choose Bible references and write a short reflection and prayer. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold. Please do not include names or sensitive details you do not want processed.

Bible text

Bible text is public domain (World English Bible, King James Version, Reina-Valera 1909, and Bíblia Portuguesa Mundial translation) and is stored in the app.

Subscriptions and analytics

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Adapty (adapty.io) processes purchase and subscription status and anonymous app-usage events (such as onboarding steps and paywall views), tied to an anonymous profile id. We do not collect your name or email.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push server is used.

Photo library

Lumvia asks for add-only access to your photo library, used only when you tap Save on a verse card.
